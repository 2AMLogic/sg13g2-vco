## Method (restated; a number without its method is not a result)

Unchanged from the pre-#79 record: ngspice's `.tran` has no device noise, so no periodogram is possible; the estimator measures the
impulse sensitivity function directly (triangular charge impulse, half-width 2 ps, at 10 carrier phases per transient; permanent excess-phase
step read from crossing-time shifts against a zero-amplitude reference run with an identical PWL breakpoint set), then
`L(df) = Gq_rms^2 * S_i / (2 (2 pi df)^2)` with `S_i` the equivalent noise current of a small-signal `noise` analysis at the DC operating
point. Plateau = last 35 % of each inter-impulse interval; threshold = reference-run window mean; discarded startup 0..2.5 ns.

**One method input was changed, and why (disclosed failed attempt).** The impulse spacing is `(3 + 1/10)` carrier periods *at a
declared nominal carrier* (`PN_F0_NOM`), which was 5.384 GHz (the pre-#79 carrier). The post-#79 reference carrier measures
**4.197 GHz**, so a train designed at 5.384 GHz spaces impulses 2.42 periods apart; the first attempt (job `klt-sim-9d3c00681207`,
kept in `isf-post79-attempt1-f0-5p384G/` with its diagnostic CSVs) had two impulses with no crossing in their plateau window and
`pn_reduce_ensemble` rejected every realisation (8 of 10 phases sampled), publishing no summary -- the gate working as designed. The
nominal is a grid-design parameter, not a measured quantity (`pn_bench.sh`), so the train was re-designed at the *measured* reference
carrier (`--f0 4.197e9`; measured carrier is -0.006 % from it) and re-run. The charge magnitudes and every other constant are
unchanged. The attempt-1 diagnostics are not used for any number below.

A second slip, caught before use: the first port-noise submission wrote both ports' decks to one filename, so the "tank" request ran the
tail probe. Its output (nonsense `inoise` ~1e-2 A/sqrt(Hz), |Z| ~ 1e-7 ohm) was discarded and both noise requests were regenerated with
per-port deck names (generator fixed); only the corrected jobs above are cited.

## Variance and its meaning

The estimator has no random input (one `.ic`, no seeds), so a Monte Carlo variance does not exist. The reported sd (0.21 dB) is the spread
across four *nuisance-parameter* realisations (sign and magnitude of the injected charge); it bounds the first-order-ISF approximation,
not process, noise or sampling variance. Process/temperature variance is not measured here (one corner).

## Limits

- **One corner.** Grades no row-4 point; the 135-point grid is not run. Hot (+125 C) rows remain the recorded HBT first-timepoint abort.
- **Port-noise referral is evaluated at the DC operating point** (the oscillator's unstable equilibrium) and treated as stationary and
  white; the cyclostationary modulation is not captured. Unchanged from the pre-#79 method and the largest stated limit. Tail-port band
  spread (34 %) is large; the tank-port number the headline uses is flat to 6.9 %.
- Only 10 phase samples per transient (max phase gap 0.70-1.09 rad vs 0.63 ideal) and c0 for the tank port is small but non-zero; c3 is
  not negligible against c2 for the +-8 fC runs, so the ISF's higher harmonics are only coarsely resolved.
- 1/f and bias-network noise (the `c0` conversion) are not in the kernel used.
- The pre-impulse null floor is an exact 0 because both decks share a timestep schedule; it demonstrates determinism, not a noise floor
  of the step readout itself. The step-readout resolution is bounded by the crossing interpolation (order 0.02 ps at the 2 ps ceiling,
  against ~1 ps steps).
- OSDI ABI actually loaded: not measured (see above).
- Inductor: the non-PDK EM-fitted model; the +-0.42 dB bar is its own stated accuracy.
