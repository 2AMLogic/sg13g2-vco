# Record 20260926-010713-e391693

- **Experiment**: oscillator-core (method check)
- **Claim**: that `sim/lib.sh`'s `osc_metrics()` and
  `osc_settle()` -- the crossing-counted frequency / peak-to-peak /
  trapezoidal-time-average and envelope-settling-time estimators every
  number in this experiment is derived through -- recover the correct
  answer on waveforms whose frequency, amplitude, time average and
  settling time are known in closed form. **Ratifies no
  `spec/target-spec.md` row and says nothing about `design/vco.sch`.**
- **Method**: an ngspice transient over five behavioural-source
  traces (`testbench/tb_period_count_known_answer.spice.tmpl`):
  pure sinusoids at 4.5e9 / 5.0e9 / 5.5e9 Hz with amplitude
  0.55 V on a 3.3 V offset; the same 5.0e9 Hz tone with an
  exponentially rising envelope of time constant 0.5e-9 s; and a
  constant 3.3 V trace as the falsification case. Run
  `tran 1p 20n` at each timestep ceiling in
  `{2p 5p 10p 20p}`; extracted over the 10e-9 s .. 20e-9 s
  window, the same window split the graded sweep uses.
- **Closed forms checked**: `f` = the stated tone frequency;
  `Vpp` = 2*0.55 V; time average = 3.3 V;
  `t_settle` = -0.5e-9*ln(1-0.9) =
  1.151293e-09 s;
  and, for the constant trace, `f` = 0 with `Vpp` = 0.
- **Tolerances**: DERIVED, not chosen -- each is a closed-form bound
  on the estimator's own discretization error at the ceiling being
  checked, times a stated 1.5x headroom, and each row's
  `tol_basis` column names its bound:
  - frequency: `dt*f/cycles` (the interpolated-crossing bound)
  - amplitude: `1-cos(pi*dt*f)` (peak-sampling error)
  - time average: `amp/(pi*cycles)` (non-integer-period residual)
  - settling time: half a period (`osc_settle` resolves to the
    nearest local extremum by construction)
- **PDK**: none, deliberately. The deck uses only ngspice-native
  behavioural sources, so this check runs on a bare checkout and a
  failing extractor cannot be confused with a PDK or OSDI problem.
- **ngspice**: `ngspice-46`
- **Result**: 56/56 checked quantities within their
  derived tolerance.
