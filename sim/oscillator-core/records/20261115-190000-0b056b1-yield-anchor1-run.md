# Anchor-1 Monte Carlo yield campaign run — seed control, process and mismatch instruments, known-bad control (issue #244, AC 4)

Append-only. Everything in this record was produced by the staged plan in
`../yield/README.md` (written 2026-10-17, updated 2026-11-15 by this run); the
requests, netlist bodies, reports, sample-set documents and `klt yield`
envelopes are committed in `../yield/`.

## What ran, in one paragraph

The anchor-1 stages of the plan (S0 + S1 + both negative controls): 16-unit
seed control, 800-unit process instrument, 800-unit mismatch instrument
(N = 400 paired Vctrl 0.0/3.3 endpoints each, seed 244, `seed_scope: shared`),
the deterministic RTE sizing probe, and the known-bad control at the same N,
seed and axes (800 + 800). All 3216 fleet units ran on ngspice-47; 3216/3216
returned measurements. `klt yield` at the repo pin (klayout-tools 0.8.0)
graded the assembled sample sets; both envelopes and the six per-measurement
sample sets are committed. Rows 1 and 2 grade as misses (yield 0.0 — the
DR-006 shortfall, sampled, not created, by this campaign); row 7 grades
400/400 under both instruments with its negative control detected. T1 item 6
now cites this evidence (`unmet`/`check_failed` with the full detail — see
"Signoff" below).

## `klt yield` grading (klayout-tools 0.8.0, the pin)

`assemble_yield_samples.py` pairs samples by `mc<index>` across the endpoints
(`seed_scope: shared` makes sample k the same die at both) and derives:
band centre f0 = geometric mean of the endpoint frequencies (row 1, limits
4.5–5.5 GHz), tuning ratio f(0)/f(3.3) (row 2, ≥ 1.15), and swing =
vdiff_max − vdiff_min at the **worse** endpoint (row 7, ≥ 0.40 V). One
sample-set document per (instrument, measurement), limits quoted from
`spec/target-spec.md`, never adjusted; run defaults (confidence 0.95,
half-width 0.01, target yield 0.99) in `yield-limits.json`.

Reading of the AC-4 gates: `sample_size.verdict` is `sufficient` on all six
measurements (observed half-width ≤ 0.01 at N = 400 — the sizing argument's
target), and the declared negative control is `detected` on both swing
envelopes. The zero-failure row-1/2 yields are the **DR-006 shortfall
sampled**: band centre 4.00 GHz against a 4.5 GHz floor and ratio 1.109
against 1.15, with the spread now measured around the miss (σ_f0 ≈ 8.5 MHz

not a refusal (docs/cli/yield.md). The sampler-injection harness check for all
measurements is the separate deterministic S0 above.

## Limits

- One corner (the PDK statistical centre), one temperature, one 20 ns window,
  400 draws per instrument, seed 244 — the anchor-1 plan, not the full
  campaign. The G1/G2/G4/G5 gaps of the plan (inductor and resistor spread
  unsampled, no joint process+mismatch section, no corner-centred sampling)
  all still apply to these numbers: row-6/7 spread in particular is
  understated by the unsampled resistor terms (G2/G5), and the two
  instruments' variances are not added (G3).
- The `selft=0` bound is measured at one bias point (Vctrl = 0, tt_nominal);
  the swing shift at Vctrl = 3.3 V is bounded by the same comparison in the
  seed control (0.52855 → 0.52631 V, −0.42 %, same direction and magnitude).
- The fleet's ngspice-47 build (2026-11-13) is not version-pinned by the
  runner (klayout-tools#5853); a future image change would be caught by the
  requests' `engine_version` pin, after the run.
- The negative control's sizing probe predates its sampling run by minutes,
  not by a re-derivation of the variant's swing distribution — the probe is
  deterministic (zero spread), which is the point.
- `keep_artifacts: false` on the four 800-unit requests: per-unit decks/logs
  were not retained (the report carries every per-sample measurement, seeds
  and digests); the 16-unit seed control retains its full artifacts.
