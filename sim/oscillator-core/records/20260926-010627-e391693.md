- **Method** (full statement, because a frequency without its method is
  not a result -- `CLAUDE.md`):
  - ngspice has no PSS/pnoise, so `f_osc` is a TRANSIENT plus period
    count, not a spectral or harmonic-balance solve. It states no phase
    noise and no line width.
  - transient `tran 1p 5n`, timestep ceiling
    `2p` (~100 samples per period at 5 GHz).
  - startup from a 10 mV differential `.ic`
    (`v(OUTP)=3.305`, `v(OUTN)=3.295`) -- an
    initial condition, never refreshed.
  - discarded startup window 0 .. 2.5e-9 s; measurement
    window 2.5e-9 s .. 5e-9 s. The discard is
    2.8x the settling time measured at the nominal corner; every point
    records its own `t_settle_s`, so a corner that needed longer is
    visible rather than silently mismeasured.
  - `f_osc` = rising crossings of `v(OUTP)-v(OUTN)` through the
    window's own trapezoidal time average, crossing times linearly
    interpolated, frequency from the first and last crossing
    (`sim/lib.sh` `osc_metrics`).
  - **frequency quantization floor**: recorded per point, both bounds
    -- `quant_floor_interp_pct` = `dt_max*f/cycles` (the estimator
    actually used) and `quant_floor_count_pct` = `1/cycles` (what
    the floor would be without interpolation).
  - `Kvco` = first differences of measured `f_osc` between
    consecutive `Vctrl` points; linearity is the peak-to-peak spread
    of the per-segment slopes as a percentage of their mean.
  - supply current recorded TWICE and never conflated:
    `isup_dc_op_a`/`p_core_dc_op_w` from the DC operating point and
    `isup_ls_avg_a`/`p_core_ls_w`, the LARGE-SIGNAL trapezoidal time
    average of `-i(VSUP)` over the settled window.
  - differential-mode check on every point: `vpp_cm_over_diff`,
    `f_cm_over_diff`, `f_tail_over_diff`.
  - row-6 margin: the tail-current-scaling threshold proxy declared in
    `testbench/tb_vco_core_margin.spice.tmpl`, reported as a BRACKET
    between the last oscillating and first non-oscillating rung. "It
    oscillated" is not reported as a row-6 result.
  - extractor validation: `run_method_check.sh`, same
    `osc_metrics`/`osc_settle`, closed-form answers, derived
    tolerances.
