## Row 6 — decision rung picked from deterministic probes, then the ladder

The per-sample startup-ladder estimator resolves a die's margin as a LOWER
bound from one rung of the pilot's RREF ladder: a die that sustains the rung
reports its measured I_nom/I_tail-at-the-rung (I_tail measured from
`vte_avg`/RTE, never inferred from the RREF multiplier); a die that fails it
reports the floor 1.0. The rung sets the resolution, so it is picked from
deterministic evidence before any sampling:

| Probe (1 unit, plain typical, Vctrl 1.65) | vte_avg (V) | I_tail (uA) | late pk-pk (V) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ladder-probe-rref1` (nominal) | 0.4065 | 203.2 | 0.5893 |
| `ladder-probe-rref2` | 0.2216 | 110.8 | 0.3056 |
| `ladder-probe-rref3` | 0.1521 | 76.1 | 0.1843 |
| `ladder-probe-rref4` | 0.1157 | 57.8 | 0.0999 — below criterion |
| `ladder-probe-rref6/8/12` | — | — | no oscillation |
| `rung-pick-rref69` | 0.1364 | 68.2 | 0.1517 |
| `rung-pick-rref71` | 0.1327 | 66.4 | 0.1435 |
| `rung-pick-rref73` | 0.1293 | 64.6 | 0.1355 |
| `rung-pick-knownbad71k` | 0.1509 | 75.5 | 0.00063 — fails the rung |

Margin-equivalents (I_nom/I_rung over RTE 2k): 69k = 2.980, 71k = 3.062,
73k = 3.145. **Decision: RREF 71k** — the strongest starvation whose measured
nominal equivalent still clears the 3.0 floor, so a sustaining die certifies
>= 3.0; 69k (2.980 < 3.0) would grade the campaign yield ~0 by estimator
construction; 73k spends its resolution further above the floor with the
least criterion headroom (0.1355 V). The nominal die sustains the criterion
at 71k (0.1435 V >= 0.1 V), and the known-bad body at this exact rung
(`vco_core_body_known_bad_rref71k.spice`) is verified to fail it (0.00063 V)
*before* the campaign was submitted. Samples between the floor and the rung's
equivalent grade conservatively as fail — the reported yield is a lower bound
on the true yield at 3.0.

Campaign: `request-r6-ladder-rref71-{process,mismatch}.json`, N = 400,
seed 244, `seed_scope: shared`, Vctrl 1.65 V (the pilot ladder's convention);
jobs `klt-sim-86ae4fdcf780` / `klt-sim-0b633818a930`, 400/400 each. Control:
`request-r6-ladder-control-rref71-{process,mismatch}.json`, N = 100 per
instrument at the same rung, seed 244; jobs `klt-sim-e8d48d9f9a4d` /
`klt-sim-b907d46874ba`, 100/100 — every control unit fails the rung, so the
assembler derives its margin floor at 1.0 and `klt yield` reports control
yield 0.0: **detected** on both instruments.

| Envelope | result |
|---|---|
| `yield-envelope-process-startup_margin_lower.json` | **pass** — yield 1.0000 [0.9908, 1], sufficient, control detected |
| first mismatch draw (seed 244) | yield 0.9125 [0.8804, 0.9383], **`sample_size.verdict = insufficient`** (observed half-width 0.0289 > 0.01; the grader requires n >= 3068 at that yield) |

The first mismatch draw's verdict is a sizing fact, not a measurement
failure. The plan's rule applies — **extend with a NEW seed, never argue
around** — and its envelope is deliberately **not committed and not cited**
(it would be the one envelope in the tree grading `insufficient`, against AC
4e's letter); its sample-set document
`yield-samples-mismatch-startup_margin_lower.json` stays committed as the

`yield-samples-mismatch-startup_margin_lower-ext245.json`; `klt yield`

- **342/4200 samples below the 3.0 floor -> yield 0.9186, CI [0.9099,
  0.9267]**, `sample_size.verdict = sufficient` (observed half-width 0.0084
  <= 0.01), negative control **detected** (yield 0.0 at N = 100; the control
  is a discriminability self-check, not a sizing claim, so it is not re-drawn
  with the extension seed). Envelope:
  `yield-envelope-mismatch-startup_margin_lower-ext245.json`, cited for row 6.
- **The two draws agree**: below-floor fraction 8.75 % (seed 244, N = 400) vs
  8.14 % (seed 245, N = 4200); mean margin-equivalent 3.0624 vs 3.0627 — no

- **Reading**: row 6 grades a **miss under mismatch on its merits** — the
  lower-bound yield 0.9186 sits below the 0.99 target. The bound is
  conservative in the direction stated above (a sustaining die's reported
  value under-states its true margin), so the miss is real at the bound's own
  semantics. As with rows 1/2 this is the DR-006 shortfall sampled, not
  caused, by this campaign; disposing it is a design decision in DR-006's
  lane and no bound is edited here.

## S4 — deterministic inductor sensitivity (gap G1's only bound)

`request-s4-inductor-l{0936,1054,1064,1077}.json`: the EM-fitted inductor
model copy with its total series L scaled by 0.936 / 1.054 / 1.064 / 1.077 —
the analytic model's measured error class against EM (issue #9's numbers) —
plain typical sections, both tuning endpoints, no sampling; bodies
`vco_core_body_selft0_l*.spice`, one-edit derivations with headers stating
the edit. Jobs in `evidence-ac4c-probes/` `set-manifest.json` (4 jobs, one
per scaling, 2 units each, 8/8 pass).

| L scaling | f(0.0 V) | f(3.3 V) | shift vs nominal f(0.0) = 4.2134 GHz |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.936 (-6.4 %) | 4.3569 GHz | 3.9262 GHz | +3.40 % |
| 1.054 (+5.4 %) | 4.1027 GHz | 3.6978 GHz | -2.63 % |
| 1.064 (+6.4 %) | 4.0830 GHz | 3.6801 GHz | -3.10 % |
| 1.077 (+7.7 %) | 4.0581 GHz | 3.6578 GHz | -3.69 % |

The shifts track 1/sqrt(L) to within 0.1 % — the sensitivity is the physical
one, no anomalous model behaviour. **The G1 bound**: even the most favourable
scaling (-6.4 % L) puts the band edge at 4.357 GHz, still below the 4.5 GHz
row-1 floor — the row-1 band-centre miss is not explainable or fixable by the
inductor model's measured error class. This is gap G1's only available bound
(the inductor has no statistical model); it is deterministic and stated at
the 27 C anchor on the `selft=0` body, with the same no-hot-corner restatement
as every other number in this record.
