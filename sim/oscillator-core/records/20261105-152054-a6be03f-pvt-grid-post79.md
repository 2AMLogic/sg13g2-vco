## Result (counts only — nothing here is graded)
| Temp | pass | error | cause of error |
|---|---|---|---|
| -40 C | 360 | 90 | `t_10_periods` "out of interval" |
| 27 C | 341 | 109 | same |
| 125 C | 0 | 450 | **all** `timestep_too_small` at the first timepoint (xq1 HBT), as in the #280 record |

- **125 C is recorded as an error with cause on all 450 units, not dropped.** This is the known first-timepoint abort
  (#280/#281); it is not fixed or explained here. The separate >52 C `dsubw` NaN (#95) was not isolated: the 125 C
  failure here is the HBT abort.
- The 199 non-hot errors are all Vctrl >= 2.4 V (3.0 V: 90, 3.3 V: 90, 2.4 V: 19). The cause is the **estimator**, not a
  demonstrated failure to oscillate: the `.meas` needs 21 rising crossings of v(vdiff) inside 5 ns and the frequency
  here is ~4.1-4.3 GHz, so the count is borderline (the README already warns this estimator is coarse).
  Whether those units oscillate was not checked from waveforms (not retained).
- For the 701 passing units, f_osc from 10 periods is 4.10-4.31 GHz overall; the spread across Vctrl 0.0-1.2 V is
  nil at the stated precision (4.18-4.31 GHz at every one of those voltages, set by process corner), and it falls only
  above ~1.65 V. **That is a descriptive observation of a coarse, 10-period, 2.5 ns-window estimator; it is not a Kvco, a
  tuning ratio or a row-2/3 verdict, and its variance was not estimated.**
